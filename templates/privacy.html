Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains what information Spendly collects, how it is used and the choices you have.

1. Data We Collect

  • Account information: your name, email address and a securely hashed password.
  • Expense data: the amounts, categories, dates and descriptions you enter.
  • Technical data: basic information such as browser type and the time of your requests, used to keep the service working.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your information to:

  • create and secure your account;
  • show your expenses, summaries and spending breakdowns;
  • maintain, troubleshoot and improve Spendly.

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored in a database associated with the Spendly application. Passwords are stored in hashed form, never as plain text. We take reasonable steps to protect your information, but no system is completely secure.

You can ask us to delete your account and expense data at any time, and we will remove it unless we are legally required to keep it.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address when pages load. We do not share your expense data with advertisers or other third parties. If we add services such as analytics or email in future, we will update this policy.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.

6. Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy or your data, contact us at the support address listed on the Spendly website.
